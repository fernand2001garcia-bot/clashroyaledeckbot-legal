Clash Royale Deck Bot

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 5, 2026

Information We Collect

The Bot only processes information necessary to provide its functionality, including:

  • Discord User IDs
  • Discord Server IDs
  • Slash command input
  • Selected Clash Royale cards

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Passwords
  • Private messages
  • Email addresses
  • Payment information
  • Personal files

Data Storage

The Bot does not permanently store deck requests or personal Discord content unless explicitly stated in future updates.

Third-Party Services

The Bot operates through Discord and may interact with official Clash Royale deck links. Users are also subject to the privacy policies of those services.

Data Security

Reasonable efforts are made to protect any processed information, but no online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children's Privacy

The Bot is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children under the applicable age requirements established by Discord.

Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be revised periodically. Changes become effective upon publication.
